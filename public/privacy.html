Privacy policy

Spend Analysis · Last updated 13 October 2026

Spend Analysis is a personal expense tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app.

What the app stores

The expenses, income, budgets and notes you enter are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are never uploaded to any server.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, share or sell any personal or financial data.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or files.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from History → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
